About

Flock Follow lets people share their live location with friends or inside groups. The mobile app is built with Flutter, the backend with Django, and Google Maps handles the tracking.

Built with

FlutterMobile app for Android
DjangoBackend API, written in Python
Google MapsLive tracking on the map
